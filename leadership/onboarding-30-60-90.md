# 30 / 60 / 90 day onboarding plan

**Role:** [role] · **Support person:** [name/role] · **Context:** [team/product]

## Days 1–30 — Context and trust

- Agree on role, responsibilities, working agreements and accessibility needs.
- Introduce key people and support channels; ensure setup/access is documented.
- Pair on one small, low-risk task; obtain feedback both ways.
- Define what success looks like with the new colleague.

## Days 31–60 — Contribution and autonomy

- Complete one scoped contribution with mentoring.
- Practice documentation, code review or other role-relevant collaboration.
- Identify obstacles and update learning plan.
- Hold regular 1:1s focused on support and expectations.

## Days 61–90 — Ownership and growth

- Own a clearly bounded outcome with realistic support.
- Review role expectations and competencies against evidence.
- Ask for feedback about onboarding and the team environment.
- Co-create the next learning goals and mentoring arrangements.
