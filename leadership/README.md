# Tech Team Development Playbook

## Purpose

A practical, evolving framework for selecting, onboarding, mentoring and motivating technology teams, informed by previous experience in people management and ongoing software training at **42 Málaga Fundación Telefónica**.

**Status:** working framework. This is not presented as evidence of running an engineering organization or as an officially endorsed 42 Málaga Fundación Telefónica methodology.

## Principles

1. Hire with consistent, job-related criteria and structured evidence.
2. Create psychological safety, clear expectations and constructive feedback.
3. Support learning through practice, mentoring and peer collaboration.
4. Evaluate growth with observable outcomes, not constant surveillance.
5. Respect privacy, accessibility and differing professional backgrounds.

## Responsible usage

These templates are examples for adaptation, not recruiting decisions or legal advice. Avoid collecting sensitive candidate data unless strictly necessary and lawful. Use human oversight, accessibility adjustments and regular checks for bias.

## What comes next

Document anonymized, consented real case studies, actual coaching contributions and lessons learned as verifiable evidence becomes available. Claims of measurable improvements should include a baseline, method and date.
