# One-to-one conversations and feedback

Use these prompts as a flexible guide for coaching and support.

1. What is going well, and what work are you proud of?
2. What has been frustrating or blocking progress?
3. Where would clearer expectations or a decision help?
4. Which skill would you like to practice next?
5. What support would make your work more sustainable?
6. What concrete action should each of us take next?

## Feedback format

- **Situation:** describe a specific context.
- **Observation:** share concrete behaviour or output, not assumptions about intent.
- **Impact:** explain the effect on others or the work.
- **Dialogue:** invite their view and clarify context.
- **Next step:** jointly agree an action, owner and check-in.

Keep personal notes minimal, private and role-related. Do not publish individual feedback or personnel records.
